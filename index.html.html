The Problem

Spanish-speaking clients are
calling. Nobody answers.

41 million people in the US speak Spanish at home. Most law firms, dental offices, and clinics have no one who can take their call professionally — in their language.

01

Calls go to voicemail

Most professional offices miss 30–40% of inbound calls during peak hours. Spanish-speaking callers who hit voicemail almost never leave a message — they hang up and call someone else.

02

Language barriers lose clients

When a Spanish-speaking client calls and no one can help them in their language, they don't wait. They find someone who can. Your competitor just got that case or appointment.

03

After-hours calls disappear

Legal emergencies, dental pain, and urgent medical needs don't follow business hours. Without after-hours coverage, every evening and weekend call is a permanently lost opportunity.

04

Intake is inconsistent

When your front desk is overloaded, intake quality drops. Wrong case info, missed details, unqualified appointments — all of it costs you time and money on the back end.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a long-term contract?
No. All plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month by delivering qualified intake and real results. Cancel anytime with no penalty or fees.
Will you answer as our firm or as Echoline?
We always answer in your firm or practice name. Clients have no idea they're speaking with a third-party intake team. It sounds exactly like your in-house front desk — in both English and Spanish.
How quickly can you get us set up?
Most clients are live within 24 hours. We configure your intake script, set up call forwarding, and connect your calendar — all before your next business day.
Are you HIPAA compliant?
We operate with HIPAA-aware intake protocols and handle all patient and client information with strict confidentiality. We recommend discussing your specific compliance requirements during your consultation so we can confirm full alignment.
What is the lead generation add-on?
After 90 days as an intake client, you become eligible for our lead generation service. We actively reach Spanish-speaking communities in your area and deliver qualified potential clients directly to your practice — for a flat monthly fee. This is separate from your intake service and subject to state bar compliance review for law firms.
What practice management systems do you work with?
We integrate with most major systems including Clio, MyCase, Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and general calendaring tools like Google Calendar and Outlook. Ask us about your specific system during the consultation.
